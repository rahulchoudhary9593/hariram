Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence in Infrastructure

Founded in 2008, DM Infra Solutions has grown from a humble local contractor to one of the most respected and reliable Government Approved PWD contractors in the state.

We specialize in large-scale infrastructure projects, including state highways, urban road networks, and specialized asphalt paving. Our success is built on a foundation of uncompromised quality, strict adherence to safety standards, and timely delivery.

  • Class-A Government Approved PWD Contractor
  • ISO 9001:2015 Certified for Quality Management
  • Over 150+ major projects completed successfully
