Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence in Infrastructure

DM (DaDaJi Maharaj) Road Work

Founded in 2026 by Hari Ram Choyal, DM Road Work was born from a vision to modernize local infrastructure with technical expertise and reliability. Despite being a new entrant, we operate with the efficiency of an established firm, prioritizing durability and safety in every project.

We are a Class-A Government Approved PWD Contractor, already setting high benchmarks in road development, rehabilitation, and specialized paving solutions.
